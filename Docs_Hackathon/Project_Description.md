## Project Description

### The Problem

Students often need to process large amounts of study material across many formats while preparing summaries, revising difficult concepts, and practicing for exams. Reading the full material repeatedly and creating practice questions manually can be time-consuming, especially for students who study independently or need additional explanations.

### Our Solution

StudyHelper provides a single desktop workspace for uploading PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown documents and working with their content through Google Gemini. The app organizes interactions into separate summary, explanation, and quiz chats, allowing students to:

- Turn study documents into clear summaries
- Receive detailed explanations using examples and simpler language
- Generate practice quizzes and check their answers
- Ask follow-up questions in context
- Revisit earlier questions through chat history

### Who Benefits

StudyHelper is designed for students and independent learners who want to understand and review course material more efficiently. It can also help tutors or educators quickly create practice questions and alternative explanations for existing study resources.
